WICKED WHISKERS — GAME SCRIPT
=============================
All dialogue and narration lines currently in the game (extracted from the
code, 2026-08-04). Edit freely — these are the words, not the logic.

NOTE: Several prompts still read "E — ..." from the old keyboard scheme.
The game now uses click-to-interact, so you may want to update those to
"Click — ...".


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. INTERACTION PROMPTS (shown near the bottom of the screen when hovering)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- "E — Talk to Dumbleclaw"              (Dumbleclaw)
- "E — A mouse"                         (field mouse)
- "E — A bird in the tree"              (bird in the tree)
- "E — The pond"                        (pond / fish)
- "E — Farm tractor"                    (tractor)
- "Click — Combination padlock"         (shed door, locked)
- "Click — Farmhouse door"              (farmhouse door)
- "Click — gate"                        (the farm gate)
- "Click — interact"                    (default fallback)

THERE SHOULD BE NO E ACTIONS, EVERYTHING SHOULD BE CLICK BASED.

2. DUMBLECLAW — FIRST MEETING (only plays once, then the choices menu)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Dumbleclaw: Ah… young Whiskers. I am Dumbleclaw, former Headmaster of Mogwarts with nearly nine lifetimes of purr?found wisdom to share. I’ve been playing with this ball for thirty?three and a third years, that must be something of a long?playing record. 
Dumbleclaw: Now then… the farm. A purr?fect playground of opportunity — flying twits perched in the trees, a nice fat fish would be a reel catch — though they can be a little koi, and you will want to be on squeaking terms with the mice. (Hope that last pun is not too cheesy.)
Dumbleclaw: if you dinner eat for a day you might lose a life but if you have any spare fare I will buy it off you for gold which will be useful later.

4. DUMBLECLAW — ADVICE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
If you've caught NOTHING yet, during the day:
 Dumbleclaw: Still nothing caught, whiskers? That’s a purrthetic showing. I have heard tales that the farmer’s wife will feed a sorrylooking cat, so you might investigate the farmhouse — but real cats hunt.
If you've caught NOTHING yet, at night:
 Dumbleclaw: No luck yet, whiskers? Those birds can be a soar point, and the fish are not much batter. Let minnow if you need a new angle. As for the mice… they are definitely fast food. Maybe there is something in the shed that can help. That and the tractor might be an interesting combination.
 
If you've caught some (lists what's still missing):
  Dumbleclaw: Well done so far Whiskers! You still have <missing> to catch.
  Dumbleclaw: Each catch is a day's food. Eat one, keep one and sell the rest to me.

If you've caught ALL THREE (mouse, bird, fish):
  Dumbleclaw: You've caught them all, whiskers! Mouse, bird AND fish. I always said you had the makings of a legend.
  Dumbleclaw: Save some and trade the surplus, and save gold. 


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. DUMBLECLAW — TRADE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
If you have NO food:
  Dumbleclaw: You’ve nothing to trade yet, Whiskers. Catch a mouse, a fish, or a flying twit first. Keep one for your own belly — but I’ll buy any purrplus you bring me.

If you have MORE than 1 food (you give 1 food, gain 1 gold):
  Dumbleclaw: Splendid job young whiskers! I'm getting too old now to cash them myself. 
  Dumbleclaw: Here are(is) <gold> gold coins for that food. Spend (it) wisely. (<gold total> gold total.)

6. THE MOUSE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Lead-in: "A plump field mouse twitches its whiskers at you:"
  [Chase the mouse!]  [Try and make a  mouse trap]  [Leave it]

Chase it:
  Toast: Prowl-prowl-prowl... the mouse bolts! Here we go!

Chase fails (you can't catch it by chasing):
  "The mouse zigzags, you pounce, and --- WHUMP --- you face-plant into the grass. It dives down a hole. From somewhere below, you hear tiny laughter." (You can't catch a mouse by chasing!)

Make a trap (no materials):
  Toast: You don't have the right materials — a trap needs string and sticks.

Make a trap (half materials):
  Toast: You only have half the materials you need for the trap.

Make a trap (trap already placed):
  Toast: Your mouse trap is already out there waiting.

Trap catches the mouse:
  Toast: SNAP! The convoluted mouse trap got him! Food +1

Click the mouse again after it's caught:
  Dialogue: The fields are quiet now — you already caught the only mouse.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7. THE POND / FISH
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Lead-in: "The pond glints in the dark. A fish swims lazily inside:"
  [Try fishing with your paw]  [Make a fishing rod]  [Leave it]

Fish with your paw:
You splash and swat, but the fish just taunts you. You are going to have to scale up your efforts, you can't reach it with a paw, you will just be floundering!

Make a rod (no materials):
  Toast: You don't have the right materials for a rod — you need string and sticks. No squidding. 

Make a rod (half materials):
  Toast: You only have half the materials you need for a rod. This is not open to De-bait.

Reel it in:
  Toast: You cast your rod... and reel in a fine fish! That's oFISHial! Food +1

Click the pond again after it's caught:
  Dialogue: The pond's quiet now. You already caught your fish and that’s a fin-tastic achievement — even though the fish was a little too chubby


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
8. THE BIRD
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Lead-in: "A cheeky bird preens in the high branches:"
  [Climb the tree yourself]  [Use a ladder]  [Hit it with the tractor]  [Leave it]

Climb yourself:
  Toast: You scramble up... wobble... and land SQUARE on your head. The bird cackles. (No low branches — you'll never catch it by climbing.)

Use a ladder (no materials):
  Toast: You don't have the right materials for a ladder — you need string and sticks.

Use a ladder (half materials):
  Toast: You only have half the materials you need for a ladder.

Use a ladder (success):
  Toast: You lash a ladder together, prop it against the trunk, and scramble up...
  Toast: Gotcha! The bird is caught. Food +1

Hit it with the tractor (no keys):
  Toast: The tractor won't start — you need the keys! (Spare set's in the shed.)

Hit it with the tractor (keys, success):
  Toast: You swing up, turn the key, and point the tractor at the tree. Chugga-chugga...
  Toast: WHAM! The tractor smashes into the tree and the bird tumbles out seeing stars,, dizzy. Food +1

Click the tree again after it's caught:
  Dialogue: The tree's empty — you already caught that bird or the bird has flown.

9. THE TRACTOR
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Lead-in: "The farm tractor:"
  [Read the number plate]  [Drive to the bird tree]  [Leave it]

Read the number plate:
  Dialogue: The tractor's number plate reads: NUM <3-digit combo>.
  Dialogue: (Remember that — it might open something.)

Drive to the bird tree (no keys):
  Toast: You can't start it — you need the tractor keys! (Spare set's in the shed.)

Drive to the bird tree (bird already caught):
  Toast: The bird's already caught. No point chugging over there.

Drive to the bird tree (bird gone):
  Toast: The bird must have flown off.

Drive to the bird tree (success):
  Toast: You swing up onto the tractor. Chug... chug... CHUG!

Tractor already rolling:
  Toast: The tractor's already rolling!

10. THE SHED DOOR (combination padlock)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Prompt: "Click — Combination padlock"

Door already unlocked:
  Dialogue: The shed door stands open. Step inside — a coil of string and the spare tractor keys are waiting in here.

Wrong combination:
  Toast: The padlock stays stubborn. (Hint: the number's on the tractor's plate.)

Right combination:
  Toast: CLICK! The padlock springs open. The shed is yours!
  Toast: Step inside to reach the string and the spare tractor keys.
