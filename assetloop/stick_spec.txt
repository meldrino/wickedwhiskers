ASSET BRIEF: WOODEN STICK for Wicked Whiskers (follows the shared style sheet and rulebook above).

A single fallen tree stick/twig for the cat to fetch. Length ~55 cm, lying FLAT on the ground with its long axis HORIZONTAL along X (height about equal to its thickness). Origin at ground under stick centre.

Design (the builder guarantees contiguity, smoothness, taper and ground contact - you decide shape and placement):
- Shaft: ONE bent_cylinder through 5 waypoints walking x = -0.26 to +0.26 (LONG AXIS HORIZONTAL - the stick lies on the ground), with small alternating z offsets (+-0.01..0.02) for a gentle organic bend and tiny y drift. Radius tapers 0.016 at the thick end to 0.010 at the thin end. sides: 24.
- One stubby side branch (cylinder) starting INSIDE the shaft about 1/3 along, pointing up-sideways at roughly 45 degrees, length ~6 cm, radius ~0.007.
- A forked mini-branch near the thick end: two short cylinders from nearly the same point splitting in a V.
- A frayed/broken tip at the far -X end: cone_tip whose base sits on the shaft end and points OUTWARD (-X), lighter wood colour, radius matching the shaft end.
- 2-3 very thin bark ridge boxes hugging/embedded in the shaft surface, slightly rotated.

Colours: bark brown #6B4A2B = (0.148, 0.068, 0.023) roughness 0.85; lighter inner wood at the frayed tip #C9A870 = (0.59, 0.40, 0.16); branch tips slightly darker.

========== JUDGE CHECKLIST ==========
1. Reads unmistakably as a wooden STICK/twig?
2. Slightly bent or irregular - NOT one perfect straight cylinder?
3. At least one side branch and a forked or frayed end?
4. Wood-brown matte bark with a lighter wood hint at a broken tip?
5. Length ~55 cm, lying on the ground plane?
6. Thickness believable for a twig (~2-3 cm) relative to length?
7. Bark ridges or surface variation visible?
8. Single coherent model - segments joined smoothly, nothing floating?
