MODE: builder
FAMILY: rock
ASSET: stone (pickup)

========== WHAT TO BUILD ==========
A single natural stone the cat can pick up. It will become a mouse-trap crafting
ingredient (alongside sticks and string), so it must read INSTANTLY as "a stone"
at gameplay distance: an irregular grey lump with a flattened resting face.

Design contract:
- ONE rock part, radius 0.05-0.07 m, sitting exactly on the ground (auto_ground on).
- STYLIZED FACETED look (house style): "facet": 0.5-0.8, "smooth": false, lumpiness 0.15-0.25,
  squash around [1.0, 0.8-0.9, 0.6-0.75], flatten 0.4-0.55, any seed you like.
- Material: use style-sheet stone palette (grey or warm-grey), roughness 0.9-0.95.
- Do NOT add extra parts. One stone, one part.

========== JUDGE CHECKLIST ==========
1. Reads as a stylized game rock: angular faceted planes, irregular silhouette - NOT a smooth potato/egg/sphere.
2. Has a DISTINCT flattened resting face visibly touching the ground plane.
3. Colour is natural dry stone (grey/warm grey), matte, not saturated, not brown bark.
4. Size believable for a cat to carry (fist-to-skull sized).
