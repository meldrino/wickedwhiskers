This iteration is a significant regression in artistic direction. While you addressed the previous "torpedo" silhouette, you have replaced it with a blocky, angular geometry that violates the core requirement of a "plump round teardrop body."

1. **Body Silhouette:** The body is no longer a smooth, organic teardrop. It features a harsh, visible "seam" or ridge running laterally around the midsection, making it look like two halves of a plastic toy glued together. This is the opposite of the "smooth shading" requirement.
2. **Primitive Soup:** The caudal fin and pectoral fins are clearly defined, sharp-edged, low-poly primitives that have not been integrated into the body mesh. They look like they were "slapped on" rather than sculpted as part of a single organic creature.
3. **Fin Geometry:** The pectoral fins remain thick, wedge-like, and lack the organic flow required for a stylized goldfish. They appear as rigid, extruded blocks.
4. **Integration:** The transition between the body and the fins is jarring. The "jagged two-tone material boundaries" are still present, as the fins lack any fillet or organic blending into the body surface.
5. **Proportions:** The head is far too blunt and lacks the soft, rounded transition into the body. The eyes, while black beads, sit on the surface like stickers rather than being integrated into the head's volume.

You have failed to move toward a cohesive, sculpted look. This model currently reads as a collection of primitives rather than a living creature.
