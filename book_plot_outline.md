# Wicked Whiskers — Full Plot Outline

## The Goal
Wicked Whiskers wants to reach the **Golden Fishbowl** in the big city — a legendary trophy said to contain the most magnificent fish ever caught. To get there, he must travel from the farm, through the village, through the town, and into the city. Each location has puzzles, characters, and chaos.

The book and game share the SAME story. Each chapter = one game scene/level.

---

## Chapter 1: The Farm (The Training Ground)
**Setting:** A farm at dusk. Garden, shed, tractor, pond, bird tree.

**Story:** WW wakes up in a garden with no memory. Meets Dumbleclaw, a wizard cat who gives quests and trades food for gold. WW learns the basics: catch animals using crafted contraptions (string + sticks), manage hunger/tiredness, earn gold.

**Key Puzzles:**
- Shed padlock (combo from tractor number plate) → get string + tractor keys
- Mousetrap (string + sticks) → catch the mouse
- Fishing rod (string + sticks + nail hook) → catch the fish
- Tractor (keys) → drive into tree, knock down bird

**Puns:** "purr fect playground", "reel catch", "koi", "oFISHial", "flying twits", "soar loser", "hawk ward"

**Ending:** WW has caught all three animals, earned gold from Dumbleclaw, and heads to the farmhouse at dawn. Dumbleclaw hints: "There is more food in the house — and the town beyond has even more. But you'll need gold to get through the gates."

**Dumbleclaw's Role:** Quest giver, tutorial, first trade. Establishes the gold economy.

---

## Chapter 2: The Farmhouse (The Milk Heist)
**Setting:** Inside the farmhouse at dawn. Kitchen, living room with goldfish bowl.

**Story:** WW enters the farmhouse. Farmer's wife feeds him milk. He finds a goldfish bowl on a rickety table but can't reach the fish. Discovers the sewing box, uses wool + ribbon as a disguise to get a second milk. Spots the breadknife, uses the dog's basket to reach the cutlery drawer, saws the table leg, smashes the bowl, catches the fish. Escapes through the front window.

**Key Puzzles:**
- Disguise (wool + ribbon) → second milk from farmer's wife
- Dog basket + cutlery drawer → get breadknife
- Saw table leg → smash goldfish bowl → catch fish

**Puns:** "ruff", "loafing", "udderly", "paw-cent", "fin-tastic", "mutt-ton", "cutting edge", "out strayed"

**Ending:** WW escapes through the window with two fish, runs back to the garden. Dumbleclaw is impressed: "You've been busy. Two fish, a belly full of milk, and a farmer's wife who'll never trust a cat with a bow again. The village is next — but be warned. Town cats have no manners, and town birds have no shame."

**Dumbleclaw's Role:** Hints at the village. Warns about birds (seagulls).

---

## Chapter 3: The Village (The High Street Heist)
**Setting:** A small English village. Butcher, bakery, fishmonger, pub (The Salty Cod).

**Story:** WW enters the village. A seagull steals one of his fish. WW gets revenge via clothesline drop. Steals chicken from the butcher. Gets fed tuna by a kind woman. Decides to stay in the village.

**Key Puzzles:**
- Clothesline drop → recover fish from seagull
- Butcher's shop → steal chicken (chase sequence)
- Kind woman → free tuna (charm mechanic)

**Puns:** "gull-ible", "fin-acceptable", "highway robbery", "paws-itively", "claw and order", "purr-litical", "fowl play"

**Ending:** WW decides to stay. He smells fish from The Salty Cod and grins. "And I've only just started." Chapter 4 should have him setting up a base of operations in the village.

**Dumbleclaw's Role:** Absent (WW is on his own for the first time). This tests whether the player/reader can handle independence.

---

## Chapter 4: The Village (Deeper) — THE GOLD RACE
**Setting:** Same village, deeper exploration. New areas: the pub, the harbour/dock, the church, the mayor's house.

**Story:** WW needs more gold to get through the town gates (Dumbleclaw's hint from Chapter 1 pays off). He discovers the village is having a "Best Catch" competition at the harbour — the winner gets a gold medal. But the competition is rigged: the mayor's cat, **Purr-cival**, always wins. WW must out-fish Purr-cival using his crafted rod, while also dealing with a new rival: **Scraps**, a street cat who knows the village inside out.

**Key Puzzles:**
- Craft a better fishing rod (find new materials: wire from the church, feather from the seagull's nest)
- Out-fish Purr-cival in the competition
- Navigate the pub (distraction puzzle: knock over glasses to create chaos, steal the key to the harbour gate)
- Befriend or outwit Scraps (choice mechanic)

**New Characters:**
- **Scraps** — a scruffy street cat, one ear torn, knows every shortcut. Becomes WW's reluctant ally or rival depending on player choice.
- **Purr-cival** — the mayor's pampered Persian cat. Smug, well-fed, cheating. The antagonist.
- **The Seagull** (returns) — now an recurring nuisance. WW must deal with it again.

**Puns:** "purr-suition", "meow-nicipality", "harbour seal of approval", "claws for concern", "tabbyogating"

**Ending:** WW wins the gold medal (or steals it, depending on choices). He now has enough gold to enter the town. But Scraps warns him: "Town is not like here. Town has rules. Town has dogs. Town has the Gatekeeper." WW heads for the town gates.

**Dumbleclaw's Role:** Appears briefly at the harbour, watching from a distance. "Gold opens doors, Whiskers. But character keeps them open."

---

## Chapter 5: The Town (The Gatekeeper's Test)
**Setting:** A bigger town. The gates are guarded by the **Gatekeeper** — a large, ancient tortoiseshell cat who demands gold AND a riddle answer.

**Story:** WW arrives at the town gates with his gold medal. The Gatekeeper takes the gold but asks a riddle: "What has whiskers but cannot feel, a tail but cannot wag, and catches things that never exist?" Answer: **a fishing net**. (WW figures this out from his fishing rod experience.) Inside the town, WW finds a bustling market, new characters, and clues about the Golden Fishbowl in the city.

**Key Puzzles:**
- Riddle at the gate (knowledge check from Chapter 1 fishing)
- Navigate the market (avoid dogs, dodge busy humans)
- Find the map to the city (hidden in the pub)
- Craft a disguise to get past the city guard

**Puns:** "gate-crasher", "riddle me this, riddle me that", "tortoise-shell of a cat", "net result", "town and country"

**Ending:** WW gets the map. The city is one more journey away. But the Gatekeeper warns: "The city is where cats go to become legends — or to become dinner. Choose wisely." WW heads for the city.

**Dumbleclaw's Role:** Sends a message via Scraps: "I believe in you, Whiskers. Now go catch something worth catching."

---

## Chapter 6: The City (The Golden Fishbowl)
**Setting:** A big city. Rooftops, alleys, the Mayor's Mansion, the harbour.

**Story:** WW reaches the city. It's overwhelming — traffic, humans, dogs, pigeons, seagulls (the same one from Chapter 3, now seeking revenge). He must navigate the city, find the Mayor's Mansion where the Golden Fishbowl is kept, and claim it.

**Key Puzzles:**
- Rooftop navigation (platforming — jump between buildings)
- Seagull rematch (the seagull has brought friends)
- Infiltrate the Mayor's Mansion (stealth puzzle)
- Claim the Golden Fishbowl (final puzzle — the bowl is locked, key is with the Mayor's pampered cat)

**New Characters:**
- **The Mayor's Cat** — a fat, lazy cat who guards the Golden Fishbowl. Not evil, just bored. Can be bribed with fish.
- **The Seagull's Gang** — a flock of seagulls led by the original thief. The final boss encounter.

**Puns:** "city slicker", "paw-litics", "golden opportunity", "fish out of water", "whisker-licking good"

**The Ending:**
WW reaches the Golden Fishbowl. But when he opens it, there's no fish inside — just a mirror. He sees himself. The "most magnificent fish" was the journey, the friends he made, the chaos he caused, and the cat he became. It's a classic "it was inside you all along" moment, but done with enough humour and self-awareness that it doesn't feel cheesy.

WW laughs. "Well, that's anticlimactic." Then he looks in the bowl again and sees Dumbleclaw's reflection behind him. The old cat has followed him all the way.

"You found it," Dumbleclaw says. "The greatest catch of all."

"What? My own reflection?"

"No. The fact that you never gave up."

WW rolls his eyes. "That's the soppiest thing I've ever heard."

"I know," says Dumbleclaw. "But it's true."

They sit together on the rooftop, watching the city lights. WW has no fish, no gold, no trophy. But he has a friend, a home (the village), and nine lives' worth of stories.

"That's oFISHial," says Whiskers.

**Final pun. Fade to black.**

## Game Integration
- Each chapter = one game level with puzzles matching the book
- Craft items carry across levels (string, sticks,钩子, etc.)
- Dumbleclaw appears at key moments as quest giver
- The Golden Fishbowl is the final collectible
- Choice mechanics in Chapter 4 (Scraps) affect the ending
- The seagull is a recurring antagonist across chapters
- Gold is earned through trades and competitions, used to unlock gates

## Tone
- Warm, funny, pun-filled
- No death, no violence (chase scenes only)
- Douglas Adams meets a children's picture book
- The cat is the hero, the humans are background noise
- Every chapter ends with a joke or a cliffhanger
